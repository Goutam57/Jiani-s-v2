Community & Stanmore pop-up

It’s no longer just about pizza. It’s about people.

What started as a few pizzas shared with neighbours has grown into something much bigger.

Jiani’s was invited and sponsored to cater for a Stanmore Place residents’ Christmas event. The response was overwhelmingly positive. What began as a one-off gathering evolved into a monthly event and eventually into a weekly pop-up driven by resident demand.

Today, Friday evenings are about more than collecting pizza. Residents meet neighbours they may never have spoken to before. Conversations start, friendships form and a stronger sense of community is created.

Investing in the next generation

Young people, real experience

Local school-age residents have volunteered to help at the pop-up, gaining first-hand experience of a small business.

Through serving customers and working as part of the team, they build communication, teamwork, customer service and entrepreneurial skills, along with confidence and self-esteem.

Food can do more than satisfy hunger. It can create conversations, build relationships and strengthen communities. That is a spirit we want to carry wherever Jiani’s goes.
