Trusted to serve

Proven event experience

Jiani’s has been trusted to cater a growing range of private events across North West London and Hertfordshire, including Stanmore Fire Station Open Day, Highgate School sports days, Holi celebrations, weddings, birthdays, corporate functions and community events.

Whether serving a small private gathering or hundreds of guests, the same standards of preparation, service and care apply.

Questions, answered

How many guests can you serve?

From intimate gatherings to large events. Tell us your guest count and we’ll recommend the right setup.

Do you cater for dietary needs?

Our menu is vegetarian-inspired and we offer vegan options. Please tell us about allergies when you enquire.

What space do you need?

We’ll confirm space and access details after your enquiry.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and busy dates.

How do I get a quote?

Use the enquiry form or call 07423 454854. We’ll reply with availability and a quote.
