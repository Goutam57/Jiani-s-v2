Our philosophy

More than pizza, it’s about people.

We craft exceptional pizzas using carefully selected Italian ingredients. But the food is only part of it.

The sight of pizzas being stretched, topped and cooked fresh creates energy around the stall. People gather, conversations start and food becomes part of the memory of the occasion. We believe food is at its best when it is shared.

The craft

Dough. Sauce. Ingredients. Fire.

01

Our Dough

Freshly made from carefully selected Italian flours and slowly pre-fermented for 48 hours: light, airy and full of flavour.

 02

Our Sauce

D.O.P. San Marzano tomatoes, fresh basil, Italian extra virgin olive oil and a touch of salt. No purée. No additives.

 03

Our Ingredients

Chosen for flavour and quality, including premium Fior di Latte for its fresh, milky flavour and beautiful melt.

 04

Our Ovens

Stone-baked at intense heat and cooked in minutes, for a light, blistered crust and a freshly baked finish.

Ronnie B · The heart behind the heat

Started in 2021, with pizza at home for family.

What began as a simple moment of joy quickly became something more. After months of learning, testing, mistakes and refinement, I created a pizza I was proud of and shared it with a few neighbours. They loved it.

A handful of pizzas became small local orders, then a pop-up, then a weekly community fixture and a growing events business.
